Access Policy

Team access is managed using role-based permissions to ensure that project information, engineering reports, and AI-generated recommendations are shared only with authorized users.

Full Access

Can manage project data, review reports, and use EngiBot. Typically assigned to system owners and lead administrators.

Engineering Review

Can review technical risks and project reports. Focused on engineering validation and decision support tasks.

Reports Only

Can view generated summaries and executive reports. Designed for stakeholders requiring high-level project awareness.
