About EngiSphere

EngiSphere is a system that helps users manage engineering projects in one place. It includes project pages, secure login, maps, reports, and an AI assistant called EngiBot.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to make project information easier to organize, view, and manage instead of keeping everything scattered across different files and pages.

Projects

Users can add projects, view project details, and follow their status.

Login System

The system uses login and registration to protect user access.

EngiBot

Users can ask EngiBot questions related to engineering projects and planning.
